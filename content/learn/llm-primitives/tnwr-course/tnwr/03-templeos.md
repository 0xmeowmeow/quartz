# 03. TempleOS

**Watch, do not read.** Twenty minutes of Terry Davis navigating his own
system. He shipped a complete OS alone by *removing* things, and the
subtraction is the entire lesson.

## Check it yourself

- [ ] Write the deletion list: everything TempleOS does not have. Minimum
      eight items. (Start: no networking, no memory protection, no multi-user,
      one address space, 640×480, 16 colours, cooperative multitasking only.)
- [ ] For each item, mark it `keep-deleted` or `must-have` for tnwr v1, and
      write one clause saying why. Any `must-have` you cannot justify in one
      clause is scope creep.

## The gotcha

> The biography is the trap. It is a genuinely affecting story and it will eat
> an afternoon, and it teaches you nothing about building this. The symptom is
> that you spend three hours on TempleOS content and your deletion list is
> still empty. The lever: write the list first, then read about him if you
> still want to.
